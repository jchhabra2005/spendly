Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we also store the expense data you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly expense tracking service — to display your expenses, generate summaries, and personalise your experience. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

All user data is stored securely and we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

4. Cookies

Spendly may use basic cookies to keep you logged in and to improve your browsing experience. These cookies do not track you across other websites and are not used for advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of the app may not function correctly as a result.

5. Third-Party Services

We do not share your personal data with advertisers or external services. If we ever integrate third-party tools (such as analytics or infrastructure providers), we will update this policy and ensure those services meet appropriate data protection standards.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below. We will process your request promptly and confirm once your data has been removed.

7. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any revisions. For significant changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify users directly via email or an in-app notice.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us at privacy@spendly.com.
